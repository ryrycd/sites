About us

A neighborhood restaurant on the water

Alcove sits on Lovejoy Wharf, next to TD Garden and North Station in Boston's West End. We host date nights, pre-game meetups, business lunches and dinner parties.

Tom Schlesinger-Guidelli, our owner, set out to open a New England neighborhood restaurant. We source from New England farmers and fishermen, with line-caught seafood, farm fresh vegetables and locally sourced meats.

Tom also curates our Wine Spectator award-winning wine list, and our bar team shakes up creative and classic cocktails. The passage into the building frames a view of the Bunker Hill Monument across the Charles River.
