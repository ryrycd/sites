About us

From a July 4th truck to Main Street

We use our homemade cookies and hand scoop ice cream to make our sandwich. The cookies come from family recipes. The ice cream is locally sourced from Rancatore’s in Lexington, made in small batches with no preservatives.

In November 2014, we added a storefront at 534 Main St in Winchester. The shop has things the trucks don’t, like frappes, sundaes and espresso products.
