About us

A neighborhood bar since 1930

We're a neighborhood bar on the street between Beacon Hill and the hospital. You'll find leather banquettes, a long bar, and a kitchen that runs from lunch to late.

We're also the official CU Football watch party bar in Boston. Every Colorado game, sound on.
