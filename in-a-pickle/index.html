About us

What's in a name?

We are a restaurant on Moody Street in Waltham, across from the common. Our traditional American breakfasts and uniquely styled sandwiches have been the backbone of our success.

Tim Burke, our owner, left a restaurant management job to open a place of his own. He had enough money to last eight months without a paycheck. He looked for a full service bar and restaurant and found nothing he could afford.

In the ninth month, broke and down on his luck, he sat on the couch, head in hand, and said, "I'm really in a pickle." Not long after, he found our place across from the common and bought it. That's how we got the name.
