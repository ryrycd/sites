Allergen-free bakery · Haymarket

Everything we bake is fully vegan and free of gluten, wheat, eggs, dairy, peanuts, tree nuts, fish, shellfish, soy and sesame. Our baked goods are also coconut free. The savory menu is not.

About us

Free of the top 9, on purpose

We keep a rotating menu of cupcakes, cake donuts, brownies, whoopie pies, chipwiches and cannoli, with breakfast and lunch served all day. We have shops in Boston, Worcester and Woburn, and we ship nationwide.
