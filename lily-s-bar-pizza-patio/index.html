About us

Lily's, in the Financial District

We're a bar, a pizza kitchen and a patio at 33 Batterymarch Street in Boston's Financial District. We make our artisan dough in house and ferment it for 24 hours. We pour great local beers on draught.

We have two newly renovated spaces: our restaurant and an urban oasis on the patio. Together they can host celebrations from 20 to 200 people, from corporate luncheons and happy hours to full restaurant buyouts.

To plan an event, email Katie, our events coordinator, at events@lilysboston.com.
