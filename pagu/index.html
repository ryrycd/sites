About us

A Cambridge story, decades in the making

The story starts with Tracy's grandmother, Chin-Fun Shiue. She opened Tokyo Restaurant in Cambridge in the 1990s, and Tracy's fondest childhood memories are from its dining room. Tracy later trained at O Ya, studied pastry at Le Cordon Bleu Paris, and cooked in San Sebastian with Chef Martín Berasategui.

We're completely nut free, and dishes are designed to be easily modified for gluten free, dairy free and vegan diets. We're proud to have been awarded a Michelin Bib Gourmand, and we owe it to our team and our guests.
