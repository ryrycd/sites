About us

Four generations in the North End

Since 2011, Pauli’s has been a go-to for overstuffed sandwiches, homemade classics and fresh salads. The meatballs are Ma’s.

Our Zagat-rated lobster rolls have been on Good Morning America and Food Network’s Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives. We’re proud to be a 2026 Best of Boston winner. Stop in and see what we’re all about.
