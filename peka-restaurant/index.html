About us

A Venezuelan kitchen on Washington Street

We are a Latin American restaurant at 304 Washington Street in Brighton. Our kitchen cooks Venezuelan dishes, along with tapas, ceviche and tacos.

The bar makes mojitos and margaritas, and we pour local and domestic beers. Some nights the room fills with salsa, merengue and bachata.

We have a patio, a lounge and space for private parties, and we cater. Sundays we serve brunch from 11:30 am to 3 pm.
