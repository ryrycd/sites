About us

Malaysian food on Moody Street

The menu runs from roti canai and charcoal-grilled satay to Prawn Mee, Nasi Lemak and Chow Kueh Teow. There are rice plates, noodle soups, fried noodles, poultry and vegetable dishes too.

If you eat vegetarian, ask us. Some dishes, like the Penang Poh Piah, come vegetarian.
