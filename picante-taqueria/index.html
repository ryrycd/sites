About us

Benjamin and Jessica's place on Boston Avenue

Picante Taqueria opened in 2018. Benjamin and Jessica Morales founded it across from Tufts University, and it has become a favorite place to eat for the students.

Benjamin came to the United States in 1993. His first job was washing dishes at a restaurant in Cambridge. He worked up to prep cook, then chef. He later ran Taco Loco and Tapatio in Somerville with his brothers.

In 2017, Benjamin and Jessica set out to build something practical with a taste of Mexican food. We wanted a happy, colorful place. Thank you from our family for stopping by.
