About us

A family deli since 1968

Sam La Grassa was born and raised in Boston's North End. He worked in local restaurants around the city, then bought a deli in 1968. That deli is what we are today.

We've been at the same location for over 50 years. With help from Sam's wife and four children, we grew from a neighborhood lunch spot into one of the largest lunch operations in Boston. Sam and his sons Rob, Ronnie and Richie work here each day we're open.

We prepare our meats in house and serve them fresh daily. Our sauces and dressings are made from scratch. Our breads and rolls are baked to our specs.
