About us

Hotpot, built at your table

We opened in Allston in 2003. Today we have two locations, here on Brighton Avenue and at 16 Tyler Street in Boston.

Hotpot is a meal you build yourself. You pick a broth, order your meats, seafood and vegetables, and make your own dipping sauce.

We have private and group dining, and we cater for up to 40 people.
