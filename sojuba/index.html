About us

Korean food and drinks on Boylston

Our menu runs from kimbap and steamed egg to jokbal, pancakes, ddok-bokghi and hot stews. We also make a Korean old school dosirak, a 70s-80s style lunch box of rice, pan-fried kimchi, ham and a fried egg.

Come after 5 pm. We're open until 2 AM.
