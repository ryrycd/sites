About us

Fire, fermentation, Southwest China

We start with fermentation and traditional technique. Then we bring in locally sourced American ingredients and Western cooking styles.

Find us at 18 Eliot St in Cambridge. Some dishes, like the Stir-Fried Bullfrog and the Live Chinese Eel, come only 10 orders a day.
