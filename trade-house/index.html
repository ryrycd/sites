About us

Provisions, then a pub, now us

Our space has long been a part of Salem’s story. In the early 1900s it was a neighborhood provisions store, just steps from a bustling wharf. By the late 20th century it had become a cherished pub.

Today we honor that legacy and keep the room familiar but fresh. Specials and events go up on Instagram and Facebook.
