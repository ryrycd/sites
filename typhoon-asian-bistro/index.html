About us

Back Bay, since 2000

We are a family-owned restaurant, and we have served Back Bay since 2000. We cook modern Asian food with street-food ideas and careful technique.

We use locally sourced ingredients and premium Japanese imports. The menu runs from sushi and sashimi to hand rolls and hot dishes.

Find us at 725 Boylston Street. Copley station is a 3-minute walk away.
